# Malcolm Hughes: Stacked Rectangles

A static composition of seven overlapping rectangles whose size, orientation, position, and color all follow from a single number sequence. Odd numbers become vertical bars, even numbers horizontal bars, and each step shifts color from blue toward red.

## Artistic inspiration

Malcolm Hughes (1920–1997) was a British artist associated with the Constructionist and Systems movements. With Jeffrey Steele he founded the Systems Group in 1969, whose members (among them Peter Lowe, Michael Kidner, and Jean Spencer) made work in which the composition is generated by a predetermined rule rather than by intuition. Hughes' work often used numerical progressions, grids, and permutations, and he was an early advocate of the computer as a tool for this kind of art during his years teaching at the Slade.

This piece is a recreation in that spirit rather than a copy of a specific work: the "system" is a sequence 1 to 7, and every visual property is derived from it.

## The system

| n | Orientation | Size | Position | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vertical | 10 × 400 | Center | Blue `#0066CC` |
| 2 | Horizontal | 400 × 20 | Center | `#3366CC` |
| 3 | Vertical | 30 × 400 | 50px right of center | `#6666CC` |
| 4 | Horizontal | 400 × 40 | 60px below center | Purple `#9966CC` |
| 5 | Vertical | 50 × 400 | 50px left of center | `#CC66CC` |
| 6 | Horizontal | 400 × 60 | 60px above center | `#CC6699` |
| 7 | Vertical | 70 × 400 | 100px right of center | Red `#CC3366` |

The bar's thickness is `n × 10` pixels. Vertical bars alternate right and left of center, and horizontal bars alternate below and above it. The rectangles are painted from 7 down to 1, so the smallest, bluest bar sits on top.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in a browser. The composition is static; there are no controls.

## Technical details

- A single 600 × 600 HTML5 Canvas drawn with the 2D context
- Vanilla JavaScript with no dependencies
- Each rectangle gets a faint `rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` outline for definition
